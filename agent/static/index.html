Bhava / House & Ring notes (click to expand) ◈ Bhava 1 midpoint fixed at top (12 o'clock); Bhava 1 is symmetric around the top. Houses count anti-clockwise — Bhava 2 upper-left, Bhava 7 at bottom, Bhava 10 right. Gold arrow = exact Lagna degree (within Bhava 1). Blue notch = same, on the Bhava ring. Bhava & Rasi rings are intentionally aligned — this is correct for Whole Sign houses, where each Bhava occupies exactly one full Rasi (0°–30° of that sign). House boundaries always coincide with sign boundaries. Center lines = inter-planetary aspects only (solid = 7th, dashed = special; color = aspecting planet; thicker + glow = mutual aspect). Empty houses excluded.  ·  Small numbers inside Rasi ring = Sarvashtakavarga score for that sign (≥28 strong, 20–27 average, <20 weak).
